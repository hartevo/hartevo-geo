# Fixing How AI Engines Describe Your Product

AI answer engines may summarize your product using outdated, incomplete, or overly generic language. Hartevo helps you find those description gaps and decide what first-party content to improve.

## The problem

Even when an AI engine mentions your SaaS, the answer may miss your main use case, confuse your audience, or describe the product in a way that does not match your positioning. This can happen when public information is thin, inconsistent, or hard to interpret.

## How Hartevo helps

- Review how AI engines describe your product across relevant prompts.
- Spot recurring misunderstandings, missing use cases, or unclear category labels.
- Prioritize content updates that clarify what your product does and who it is for.
- Create or improve pages that answer the questions AI engines and buyers are likely to ask.

## Content areas to inspect

- Your homepage and product description.
- FAQ pages that answer practical buyer questions.
- Use-case pages that explain concrete problems your product solves.
- Comparison and alternatives pages that fairly define when each option fits.
- Documentation or help content that explains features in precise language.

## A practical workflow

Start by collecting the prompts where your product should be understood correctly. Use Hartevo to inspect the answers, note recurring inaccuracies, and map each issue to a content fix. Then update the relevant first-party pages and continue monitoring to see whether descriptions become clearer over time.
