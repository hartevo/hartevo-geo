# Hartevo FAQ

## What is Hartevo?

Hartevo is a SaaS tool for indie developers and SaaS founders who want to understand whether AI answer engines cite their product and how those engines describe it.

## What problem does Hartevo help solve?

Hartevo helps you track prompts where your SaaS should appear, review how AI engines mention your product, and identify content or positioning gaps that may affect AI-search visibility.

## Which AI answer engines does Hartevo focus on?

Hartevo is built around visibility in AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Coverage can evolve as AI-search behavior changes.

## Is Hartevo a replacement for traditional SEO?

No. Hartevo is focused on AI-search visibility, while traditional SEO focuses on search engine rankings, technical SEO, backlinks, and organic traffic. Many teams may use both.

## Can I track prompts manually instead of using Hartevo?

Yes. Manual prompt tracking can work for small checks or early research. Hartevo is intended for teams that want a more structured way to monitor prompts, compare answers, and turn findings into fixes.

## Does Hartevo guarantee that ChatGPT or other AI engines will cite my product?

No. No tool can guarantee citations from AI answer engines. Hartevo can help you measure visibility, understand how your product is represented, and improve the content signals you control.

## Who is Hartevo best suited for?

Hartevo is best suited for indie developers, SaaS founders, and small teams that care about whether AI answer engines mention their product in relevant buying, comparison, or recommendation prompts.

## What kinds of fixes might Hartevo help identify?

Hartevo may help identify missing FAQ content, unclear positioning, weak comparison pages, absent use-case pages, or inconsistent descriptions that make it harder for AI engines to understand your product.
