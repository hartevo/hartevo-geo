# Hartevo Alternatives

If you want to understand how AI answer engines mention your SaaS, there are a few practical approaches. Hartevo is one option, but manual prompt tracking and traditional SEO may also be useful depending on your stage, budget, and goals.

## Options at a glance

| Option | Best fit | Tradeoffs |
|---|---|---|
| Hartevo | Indie developers and SaaS founders who want a structured way to monitor AI-search visibility and turn findings into content fixes. | Focused on AI answer engine visibility, so it should complement rather than replace broader SEO, analytics, or brand research. |
| Manual prompt tracking | Founders doing early research, occasional checks, or a small number of prompts. | Flexible and low process overhead, but it can become inconsistent or time-consuming as the prompt set, engines, and competitors grow. |
| Traditional SEO | Teams focused on Google search traffic, technical site health, keyword rankings, and long-term organic acquisition. | Important for discoverability, but it does not always show how AI answer engines summarize, cite, or compare your product. |

## When Hartevo is a good fit

- You want to know whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or similar engines mention your SaaS for relevant prompts.
- You need to see whether AI engines describe your product accurately.
- You want help translating visibility gaps into practical content work, such as FAQ, comparison, alternative, or use-case pages.
- You are a small team that wants a repeatable process instead of ad hoc screenshots and notes.

## When manual prompt tracking may be enough

Manual tracking can be a reasonable starting point if you only need to test a few prompts, validate whether a category is worth monitoring, or gather qualitative examples. It is most useful when the process is simple enough that you can repeat it consistently.

## When traditional SEO should be the priority

Traditional SEO is still important if your main goal is organic search traffic from search engines, improving technical site health, or building content around search demand. For many SaaS teams, AI-search visibility and SEO are related but not identical workstreams.

## How to choose

Choose the approach that matches the decision you need to make. If you need occasional qualitative checks, manual tracking may be enough. If you need broader organic growth, traditional SEO is essential. If you need a focused workflow for monitoring and improving AI answer engine visibility, Hartevo is designed for that use case.
