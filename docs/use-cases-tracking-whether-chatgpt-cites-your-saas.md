# Tracking Whether ChatGPT Cites Your SaaS

When potential customers ask ChatGPT for software recommendations, comparisons, or alternatives, your SaaS may be mentioned, ignored, or described inaccurately. Hartevo helps founders turn that uncertainty into a repeatable monitoring process.

## The problem

Manual checks are easy to start but difficult to maintain. You might test a prompt once, save a screenshot, and then lose track of whether the answer changes later. It is also hard to know which prompts are worth monitoring and whether your product appears in the contexts that matter.

## How Hartevo helps

- Track prompts that are relevant to your SaaS category, use cases, and buyer questions.
- Review whether AI answer engines mention or cite your product in those contexts.
- Compare how your product is described across engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.
- Identify gaps where clearer first-party content may help engines understand your product.

## Example prompts to monitor

- What are the best tools for my specific SaaS category?
- What are alternatives to a known product in my market?
- Which tools help with a specific use case my product supports?
- How does my product compare with another option?

## What to do with the findings

If ChatGPT does not cite your SaaS, the next step is not to force mentions. The practical next step is to improve the factual, useful content you control: product explanations, FAQs, comparison pages, alternatives pages, and use-case pages. Hartevo is designed to connect monitoring with those fixes.
