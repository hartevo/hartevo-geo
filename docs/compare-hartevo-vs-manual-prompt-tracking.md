# Hartevo vs Manual Prompt Tracking

Hartevo and manual prompt tracking both help you understand how AI answer engines respond to questions about your market. The right choice depends on whether you need a lightweight research habit or a more structured visibility workflow.

## Summary

| Approach | Best for | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Hartevo | Monitoring repeated prompts, reviewing AI descriptions of your product, and turning gaps into content actions. | Requires adopting a dedicated workflow for AI-search visibility. |
| Manual prompt tracking | Small prompt sets, one-off research, and early exploration before committing to a process. | Can be harder to keep consistent across time, engines, prompts, and team members. |

## Who Hartevo is for

Hartevo is for indie developers and SaaS founders who want to track whether AI answer engines cite their product, compare how answers change, and identify content improvements that may help engines understand the product more clearly.

## Who manual prompt tracking is for

Manual prompt tracking is for teams that want to ask prompts directly in AI tools, save examples, and make qualitative judgments without a dedicated system. It can be a practical first step when you are still learning which prompts matter.

## Real tradeoffs

- Manual checks are flexible, but they depend on consistent note-taking and repeatable prompt selection.
- Hartevo adds structure, but it is most useful when AI-search visibility is important enough to monitor over time.
- Manual tracking may be enough for a very small product or a short research project.
- Hartevo may be a better fit when you need to connect observations to content fixes and distribution work.

## How to decide

Start with manual prompt tracking if you only need a few examples or are still defining your category. Consider Hartevo when you have a set of prompts that matter and want a repeatable way to monitor visibility, review descriptions, and improve the pages AI engines can reference.
