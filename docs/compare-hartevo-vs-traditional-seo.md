# Hartevo vs Traditional SEO

Hartevo and traditional SEO address related but different discovery problems. Traditional SEO helps you improve visibility in search engines. Hartevo focuses on how AI answer engines cite and describe your SaaS.

## Summary

| Approach | Best for | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Hartevo | Tracking AI answer engine visibility and improving content that helps engines understand your product. | It is focused on AI-search visibility, not the full range of SEO tasks. |
| Traditional SEO | Improving organic search visibility through technical SEO, keyword research, content, internal linking, and authority building. | It may not directly show whether AI engines cite your product or describe it accurately. |

## Who Hartevo is for

Hartevo is for SaaS founders who want to know whether AI engines mention their product in relevant recommendation, alternative, and comparison prompts. It is especially useful when you need to inspect answer quality, not just page rankings.

## Who traditional SEO is for

Traditional SEO is for teams that need organic traffic from search engines and want to improve site crawlability, content relevance, rankings, and search demand coverage. It remains an important growth channel for many SaaS companies.

## How the work differs

- Traditional SEO often starts with search queries, rankings, and pages. Hartevo starts with AI prompts, answer behavior, and citation patterns.
- Traditional SEO may prioritize technical fixes and keyword-led content. Hartevo may prioritize clear product explanations, FAQ pages, comparison pages, alternatives pages, and use-case content.
- SEO performance is often evaluated through search visibility and traffic. AI-search visibility is evaluated through whether and how answer engines mention your product.

## Using both together

For many SaaS teams, the practical answer is not either-or. Traditional SEO can help make your site discoverable and authoritative, while Hartevo can help you understand how AI answer engines interpret and cite your product. The overlap is strongest when your content is clear, factual, and useful to both humans and machines.
